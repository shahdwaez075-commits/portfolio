I'm a Data Analyst

I’m a Data Analyst who enjoys diving into messy datasets, discovering patterns, and turning numbers into clear, meaningful stories. I’m passionate about understanding what the data is really saying and finding insights that make a real difference.

I use Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data visualization to analyze data, build interactive dashboards, and solve real-world problems. I’m particularly interested in understanding the “why” behind the numbers — going beyond what happened to discover trends, relationships, and insights that truly matter.

I believe great analysis is not just about presenting numbers, but about turning raw data into actionable insights and better decisions. I continuously learn, experiment with new datasets, and build projects that strengthen my analytical thinking.
